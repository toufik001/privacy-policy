Last updated: 27-04-2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile application “Wessali” ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our delivery management application.

By using the application, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Introduction

Wessali is a mobile application designed to manage delivery activities, including scanning parcels using QR codes or barcodes, tracking deliveries, and managing logistics operations.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding how your data is handled.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information Provided by the User

The application may collect information that you manually enter, such as:

  • Parcel references
  • Delivery information
  • Operational data related to logistics activities

2.2 Automatically Collected Data

We may collect limited technical information such as:

  • Device type
  • App usage data (for performance and debugging)

This data is anonymized and used only to improve the application.

3. Camera Permission

Our application requires access to your device camera for the following purpose:

  • Scanning QR codes and barcodes on parcels to identify and process deliveries

Important:

  • The camera is used only when the user actively scans a code
  • No photos or videos are recorded or stored
  • No camera data is transmitted to our servers
  • All scanning is processed in real-time

4. Data Usage

We use collected data to:

  • Enable parcel tracking and delivery management
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Ensure proper functioning of core features

We do NOT:

  • Sell your personal data

5. Data Storage and Security
