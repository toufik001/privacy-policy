We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding how your data is handled.

3. Camera Permission

Our application requires access to your device camera for the following purpose:

* Scanning QR codes and barcodes on parcels to identify and process deliveries

Important:

* The camera is used only when the user actively scans a code
* No photos or videos are recorded or stored
* No camera data is transmitted to our servers
* All scanning is processed in real-time
